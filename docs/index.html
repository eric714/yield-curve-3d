Height is the annualised yield a US Treasury security of that maturity paid on that day. The near edge is short-dated debt, the far edge is the 30‑year bond.

Reconstructed data. The Treasury stopped issuing the 30‑year bond between February 2002 and February 2006, and did not publish a 1‑month rate before July 2001. Those gaps are filled by interpolation so the surface stays continuous, and shaded paler where that has happened.

Yields: US Department of the Treasury. Policy rate, balance sheet and market series: Federal Reserve Economic Data, St. Louis Fed. Both are US government works and carry no copyright.
